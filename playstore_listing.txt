🟧 Scramblox — The Word Puzzle That Stacks Up!

Scramblox takes word scrambling to the next level. With a bold neon style, explosive gameplay, and 3 modes to master, this is the word game you never knew you needed.

─────────────────────────────────────
🎮 THREE MODES
─────────────────────────────────────

🧩 CLASSIC — Pick any of 21 categories and unscramble 100 randomly shuffled words per session. Stars and coins for every solve.

⛓ CHAIN — Each solved word must begin with the last letter of the previous one. Miss and lose a life. 3 lives per round.

⚡ BLITZ — 60 seconds of pure word chaos. Combo streaks, freeze power-ups, and personal high scores.
